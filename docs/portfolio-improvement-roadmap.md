## 🎯 **Priority 2: HIGH IMPACT, MEDIUM EFFORT (Week 3-4)**

### 2.1 Case Study Business Context Enhancement
**Impact:** 🔴 **HIGH** (Core credibility for decision-makers)
**Effort:** 🟡 **MEDIUM** (8-12 hours)

**Add to each case study:**
1. **Company Profile Box:**
   - Industry and company size
   - Typical annual revenue range
   - Marketing challenges context

2. **ROI Emphasis Section:**
   - Before/after metrics prominently displayed
   - Cost savings calculations
   - Revenue impact projections

**Example Template Addition:**
```
🏢 COMPANY CONTEXT
Industry: Healthcare Technology | Company Size: 50-100 employees | Annual Revenue: $10-25M

💰 BUSINESS IMPACT
• Reduced customer acquisition cost by 35%
• Increased marketing qualified leads by 60%
• Saved 15 hours/week in manual campaign management
• Projected annual revenue increase: $2.3M
```

**Impact:** 🔴 **HIGH** (Trust building for business audience)
**Effort:** 🟡 **MEDIUM** (4-6 hours)

**Current Issue:** Testimonials buried, not business-focused enough

**Solutions:**

- Add role-specific testimonials (CMOs, Marketing Directors)
- Include industry context for each testimonial
- Add company logos (with permission)
