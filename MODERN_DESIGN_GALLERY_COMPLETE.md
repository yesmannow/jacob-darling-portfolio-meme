## 🎭 Animations & Interactions

### **Page Load Sequence**
1. **Hero fades in** (1s duration)
2. **Title animates up** (0.8s, 0.2s delay)
3. **Subtitle follows** (0.8s, 0.4s delay)
4. **Category pills appear** (0.6s, 0.6s delay)
5. **Pills stagger in** (0.05s between each)
6. **Gallery fades in** (0.5s)
7. **Cards stagger** (0.03s between each)

### **Card Hover Effects**
- **Lift:** 8px upward movement
- **Scale:** 1.02x expansion
- **Border:** Glows with category color
- **Image:** Zooms 1.1x smoothly
- **Overlay:** Info fades in from bottom
- **Gradient:** Category color overlay appears

### **Category Switching**
- **Exit animation:** Opacity 0 + move up 20px
- **Enter animation:** Opacity 1 + move from 20px down
- **All cards re-animate** on filter change
- **Smooth transition** (0.5s duration)

### **Lightbox Experience**
- **Backdrop blur:** 0px → 20px
- **Modal springs in:** Scale 0.8 → 1.0
- **Image reveal:** Smooth fade + scale
- **Details fade in:** 0.2s after image
- **Close button:** Rotates 90° on hover

## 🎨 Design System

### **Color Palette**
- **Background:** Purple-dark gradient (#1a0e2e → #0d0d0d)
- **Primary:** Pink gradient (#f093fb → #f5576c)
- **Accent:** Category-specific colors
- **Text:** White with varying opacity (0.6-1.0)
- **Borders:** Semi-transparent pink

### **Typography**
- **Title:** 3-6.5rem, weight 900, gradient fill
- **Subtitle:** 1.1-1.5rem, weight 300
- **Card titles:** 1.25rem, weight 700
- **Badges:** 0.75rem, weight 600, uppercase

### **Spacing**
- **Hero padding:** 6rem top, 4rem bottom
- **Gallery padding:** 4rem vertical, 2rem horizontal
- **Grid gaps:** 1.5rem between cards
- **Border radius:** 20px (cards), 50px (pills), 15px (CTA icon)

### **Effects**
- **Glassmorphism:** backdrop-filter blur(10-20px)
- **Shadows:** Multi-layer with color tint
- **Gradients:** 135° angle throughout
- **Blur:** 120px for hero orb, 20px for modals
